Ask to try it early How it works

Track your wealth in one place.

Add your accounts, your savings, the cash at home and the other things you own. Nordram adds them up into one figure and keeps its history, so you can watch the line move.

  • Savings

    Money you have set aside, kept apart from your main account, with a value you update yourself.

  • A negative balance is normal

    It only means Nordram has not yet seen where the money came from.

  • Fix the past

    Set what an account held on a past day, and correct it later.

What is inside

The analytics are the product, so most of the app is the part that reads your money back to you.

  • Spent and money in

    This month against last, by category and by place, with the day-by-day shape underneath.

  • Currencies at the day’s rate

    The dram first, and the foreign currencies that come with living in Armenia. Every payment keeps the currency it happened in, at the rate of the day it happened — not today’s.

  • Monthly budgets

    A limit for any category you choose, and how much of it is left this month.

  • Trips

    Tag the payments from a journey and see what a country actually cost you, on a globe you can spin.

  • Categories and places

    Nordram suggests a category for a shop it does not know, and well-known shops show their own logo. Make your own categories, each with its icon, and say where a payment you typed in happened.

Four ways to add a payment.

Use whichever is closest to hand. Every payment ends up on the same charts, however it came in.

  • A screenshot of your bank app

    The list of payments, the way your bank shows it.

  • A statement

    The PDF or CSV file your bank gives you.

  • A photo of a receipt

    The paper slip from the shop.

  • By hand

    One payment at a time, typed in. Nothing is read, so it is the most private way.

What we keep, and what we never do

Tap Get started. That is the sign-up.

No email address, no phone number, no identity check, and nothing that asks who you are. We are not connected to your bank and never will be without you doing it yourself.

  • Start as a guest

    No username, no password. Nordram gives you a name to begin with. A guest can make five imports; typing payments in is never limited.

  • Create an account

    Pick a username and a password, so you can log back in on any phone. Everything you have added stays.
