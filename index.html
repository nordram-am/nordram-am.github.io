How an import goes

Four steps, and you can stop at any of them. Nothing is saved until you say so.

Pick your screenshots

As many as you like, straight from your photos. Anything that shows a list of transactions.

Check them

You see the shots you chose before a single one is sent, and you can drop any of them.

We read them

Every amount, date and place comes back as a row. The picture itself is never kept.

Look, then keep

Untick a row you don’t want, fix a detail, keep the rest. If we weren’t sure of something, the row says so.

What is inside

The analytics are the product, so most of the app is the part that reads your money back to you.

Wealth over time

Everything you hold, in one line, across every account and pocket you have told us about.

Spent and money in

This month against last, by category and by place, with the day-by-day shape underneath.

Four currencies

Armenian dram, dollars, euros and roubles. Every payment keeps the currency it happened in, at the rate of the day it happened — not today’s.

Budgets

A limit per category, and an honest read on how much of the month is left against how much of the money is.

Pockets

Set money aside without moving it at your bank. What is left to spend stops pretending your savings are spendable.

Trips

Tag the payments from a journey and see what a country actually cost you, on a globe you can spin.
